# Native screenshot provenance

Both `windows-share.png` and `windows-compact.png` are unchanged client-area captures of the native FastCast Windows appearance-review executable, taken September 7, 2026 after a successful build. An isolated preferences file selected its default Cyan theme, compact controls, and Economy effects. Windows captured only the client area, so the operating-system title bar and its sample-application title are absent. No screenshot colours, controls, text, conversations, or session state were painted or generated.

- Wide capture: 1770 × 1170 pixels.
- Smaller window: 998 × 764 pixels. The native scroll area is visible.
- These show the development interface, newer than the public 0.3.1 Preview 1 package.
- Neither image depicts a connected media session or establishes streaming performance.
- Website image frames, labels, and zoom controls are HTML/CSS outside the screenshots.
- `og.png` is a generated typographic brand graphic for social previews, not a screenshot.
