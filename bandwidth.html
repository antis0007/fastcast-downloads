DIRECT PEER ROUTE

Sender → viewer

Sender uploads 5.40 GB. Viewer downloads that same payload. No central relay needs to receive and forward it.

HYPOTHETICAL MEDIA RELAY

Sender → relay → viewer

The relay receives 5.40 GB and sends 5.40 GB: 10.80 GB combined ingress and egress. Provider billing can count these differently.

The arithmetic.

GB = Mbps × minutes × 0.0075

One megabit is 1,000,000 bits; eight bits make one byte. Multiply by 60 seconds per minute and divide by 1,000,000,000 bytes per decimal GB.

The model assumes a constant video bitrate. It excludes audio, protocol overhead, retransmissions, error-correction traffic, and VPN overhead. Actual traffic can be higher; variable encoding changes the result. Upload and download are the same payload counted at different endpoints.
