## Rejected draft rewrites

- “Yes? Most people knock on the door.” — mild interaction banter, not an improvement worth adding.
- “I lit his candle. He wants me to pay for his eyebrows.” — a tidier version of the same stock fire gag.
- “My apprentice called me old. I gave him my knees.” — the causal connection is better, but it remains a manufactured quip.
- “I taught my front door to recognise threats. It has locked me out.” — a familiar backfiring-instruction joke.
- “A man paid me in turnips. They’re following him home.” — a consequence was supplied, but it was not interesting enough.
- “That’s a painted triangle. You can press harder, but I painted it quite thoroughly.” — strained elaboration of a nonfunctional play symbol.
- The six-hundred-year-old melon revision — fixes continuity, not comedy.
