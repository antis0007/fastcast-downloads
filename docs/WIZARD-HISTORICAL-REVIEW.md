# FastCast wizard: historical archive and fresh review

Reviewed 13 September 2026. Baseline: published repository `f33d0a6`.

**616 distinct historical wordings are preserved below, with an individual verdict and reason for each. The live 21-line pool is unchanged.**

## What went wrong

The problem was not simply excess length or a shortage of punchlines. Too much of the writing adopted the manner of a dry, eccentric wizard without giving him an interesting thing to say. A named vegetable, a random weekday, a committee, or a talking object repeatedly stood in for an observation. Closers such as “it knows why,” “you will notice,” and “we have not discussed this” concealed the part of the story that might actually have been funny.

The reviews reinforced this. They praised parallel verbs, concrete nouns, and a complete setup/payoff as if those established quality. They do not. “The Tower bills per head. My hydra is furious” is intelligible but obvious. “I revoked their stairs” has an understandable action but a strained verb chosen for symmetry. The damp sleeves are specific; that does not make the threat exceptional. My earlier keep decisions were too permissive.

Mechanical shortening also did harm. The Thursday warning lost its distinction between summoning and banishing. The long “nothing happens” Xyzzy explanation became shorter explanations of the same absence. Four versions of buffering, baths, and bureaucratic Fireballs are not four developed ideas. Contractions help speech sound natural only after there is something worth saying.

There was a second mistake in the opposite direction: treating every line without a punchline as worthless. “Are you... holding my hand?” can simply acknowledge the gesture. A small expression can be an expression. Some of the melancholy drafts mean something, but introduce an emotional relationship this page has not earned. These are different judgements from declaring the text meaningless.

## Material worth a separate audition

Only four receive Candidate rather than Borderline. Each uses magic to accomplish a particular human purpose: provoking a correction, keeping a ladder steady, interrupting a rival’s reading, or avoiding a duel. That is a reason to audition them, not a licence to generate twenty more sentences with the same structure.

- [W253](#w253): I mispronounce forbidden names until somebody corrects me.
- [W286](#w286): I summoned a demon to hold my ladder. It offered me a kingdom. I pointed at the ladder.
- [W295](#w295): I use Mage Hand to turn my rival's pages before he's finished.
- [W373](#w373): My rival demanded a duel at dawn. I have postponed the sun.

No replacement pool is proposed by this document. The next useful writing decision is whether any of these actually sounds right to the owner. Quantity targets for hat, beard, or orb should not force another batch of weak filler.
