The route from
screen to viewer.

Your Windows PC encodes a screen or window and sends it straight to the device that watches. The route has to be reachable from both ends, and the invitation that authorises it is written by the viewer — not by us.

Managed media relay

Used only when a direct route fails

A direct session never touches it. When both peers allocate one, the FastCast relay forwards the media instead. It admits eight concurrent relayed sessions. A relay you already run, such as a VPN or mesh service, is a separate cost and a separate provider.

The other
connections.

The website, downloads, and the issue tracker are GitHub. Whoever you used to pass the invite has their own logs. Your ISP sees that two machines talked, even if the payload is encrypted.

A mesh or VPN you already pay for may relay packets. That is their terms, not ours.

Direct media is not “no data anywhere.” The person watching can still record the screen.
