The current preview

Screen sharing between your devices.

0.3.2 Preview 2 is the public download. Matching Windows and Android builds, a private invitation, and a reachable LAN or private-mesh route are the supported path.

SHIPPING NOW

What you can try

  • Windows screen or window capture to Android or another Windows PC
  • Private, expiring session invitations
  • Computer audio and optional remote input, subject to device support
  • Local profiles and contacts on Windows
OUT OF SCOPE

What stays out of this preview

There is no FastCast-hosted media relay and no Discord or Teams replacement. Cross-internet connections need a route you already run, and continuity over a long session is still being measured. Hardware support beyond the tested path is unconfirmed.

The next engineering gates

Prove the current path.
Then widen it.

Work is ordered so ordinary screen sharing stays the fallback. A passing build is not device acceptance.

01

Matched-device playback and admission

Confirm picture, sound, expired or revoked invitations, and remote-input release on real Windows and Android hardware. Session admission is implemented; physical regression is still required.

Capability status →
02

Discovery you can refuse

Receivers can now turn off STUN lookups, router mapping, or both, and a machine-wide override can force that choice for headless use. Packet-capture proof that a disabled receiver sends neither request is still outstanding, so this is present in source rather than claimed for the published package.

Privacy notes →
03

Automatic connectivity

Same-socket STUN and optional router mapping exist. Coordinated hole punching, a deployed relay, and unrelated-network playback have not passed their acceptance gates.

A smaller-group direction

Planned, not downloadable.

Authenticated accounts, recoverable devices, synchronized watching of a local file, and consenting multi-viewer rooms are the longer product direction. They need membership, independent audio, and measured reliability before they belong on a download button.

Website comparison tables will stay “Not measured” until a public evidence index exists. Help shape the sequence through the issue tracker, without posting invitations or private screen content.
