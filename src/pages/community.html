How this site is written.

Say what the binary does

No invented dashboards and no invented user counts. If a result came from one LAN run, the page says so next to the number.

No telemetry here

This website has no analytics, no cookies, and no accounts of its own. Downloads are served by GitHub, which sees them; we do not.

Free today

The current Windows and Android packages carry no subscription, and there is no billing form to fill in.
