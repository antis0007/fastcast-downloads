The other
connections.

The website, downloads, and public feedback use GitHub. The service you use to pass an invitation has its own data practices. Your network operator can observe connection metadata even when transport content is encrypted.

A private-network provider may help devices find one another or relay their traffic. Check that provider’s privacy practices when choosing a route for an off-LAN session.

Local settings and diagnostics can remain on your devices. FastCast’s direct media path is not a blanket “no data anywhere” claim.
