01 / Why another app?

Communication should bring you closer.
It shouldn’t make you more dependent.

If you’re tired of subscriptions, crowded platforms, and data collection you can’t easily see, wanting an alternative is reasonable. The usual screen share is bolted onto a chat company.

FastCast starts with screen sharing. Your devices handle the media. There is no FastCast-managed media relay in this preview — and this is not Discord.

03 / A model you can understand

Free, with the costs
in plain view.

Your hardware and connection do the work. The current preview has no subscription, and it provides fewer hosted services. That tradeoff should be visible.

Your devices

The sharing PC captures and encodes. A Windows or Android viewer decodes and displays.

Your connection

Start on a LAN or reachable private network. Data caps, electricity, and any VPN service remain your costs.

A smaller hosted footprint

GitHub serves the website and downloads. A direct session needs no FastCast media relay.
