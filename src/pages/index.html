Connections

Direct connections
and relay fallback

Your screen goes straight to the other device whenever the two can reach each other. When they cannot, the encrypted video travels through a relay instead.

{{SCRYING_ORB}}

Your PC encodes the picture and the watching device decodes it. A relay only forwards the packets it is given; it never opens, plays or re-encodes them.

Pairing and discovery messages help the two devices find each other. They travel separately from the screen and audio.

Start on your own network.

Earlier builds were tested on one local network. This build still needs playback confirmed on real hardware, locally and across networks. Where UDP is blocked outright there is no media fallback.

Why {{PRODUCT}}

Screen sharing
without a new platform

Your group already has somewhere to talk, and {{PRODUCT}} does not ask anyone to leave it. Open it for the screen, keep the conversation where it is, and when the direct route works the video passes between your two devices and nowhere else.

When you would rather stay in one place, the session also carries voice calls, rooms for written conversation, and file transfer the other side accepts by hand. All three are preview work, and what they cannot do yet is written down rather than left out.

Help

Common setup
questions

First sessions usually fail for one of three reasons: the two builds are different versions, guest Wi-Fi is keeping the devices apart, or the invitation has already been used. Check those before filing a bug.

The devices won't connect.

Check that both are on the same version and can actually reach each other — guest Wi-Fi often isolates devices that share a network name. Then create a fresh invitation on the watching device and import it again. Connection checklist.

Is this remote control, or watching?

Watching, unless you turn remote input on. That switch is experimental, and nobody has measured whether it keeps up well enough for games.

What do I need before installing?

A Windows x64 PC to send, and a second Windows PC or an Android 8+ device to watch. The Windows installer is unsigned and the APK is debug-signed, so compare the published checksum before you run either. Install notes.
