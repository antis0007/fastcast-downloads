02 / Why another app?

Communication should bring you closer.
It shouldn’t make you more dependent.

If you’re tired of subscriptions, crowded platforms, and data collection you can’t easily see, wanting an alternative is reasonable. The usual screen share is bolted onto a chat company.

FastCast starts with screen sharing. Your devices handle the media. There is no FastCast-managed media relay in this preview — and this is not Discord.
