Audio and remote-input
permissions

Choose whether to share computer audio, enable your microphone, or allow mouse and keyboard control. Computer audio has historical LAN evidence; this build still needs physical-device checks for audio and remote input.

Computer audio Available

The sending PC’s own sound travels with the picture. A Windows viewer decoded it in the LAN run; audible output on a phone was not independently confirmed.

Microphone voice Preview

Microphone voice is optional and supports two-way communication. Automated checks pass, but physical round-trip and echo testing remain outstanding.

Remote input Preview

Allow the watching device to control the mouse and keyboard. Physical input and behaviour inside native games remain unverified.
