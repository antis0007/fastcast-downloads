{{PRODUCT}} 0.3.3 Preview 2

In progress. No package exists for this version yet, so the current download below is unchanged.

What changed

  • Room discussions moved to a three-column layout: rooms on the left, the conversation in the middle, members on the right. Both side rails fold away below 720 pixels wide.
  • Messages from the same person within five minutes group under one header, so a burst reads as one block instead of five.
  • A “New messages” line marks where you left off and holds its position while you read it. “Jump to latest” appears once you scroll back.
  • Hovering a message offers Quote and Copy.

Not in this work

  • No reactions, editing or deleting. The coordination service has no operation for them, and a control that did nothing would be worse than none.
  • No unread counts on rooms you are not currently reading, for the same reason.
