TESTING LIMITS

Connection and playback limits

Direct routes and a {{PRODUCT}} relay fallback are implemented. This is not a Discord or Teams replacement, and there is no guarantee of 60 fps, freeze-free playback, or automatic connections across the public internet. Physical soak tests for continuity and sound remain open.

Candidate work

Next major release

Several app changes are being integrated and reviewed. They are not in the current download, and their final scope and version are not set.

01

A clearer desktop and conversation experience

Active work brings a consistent visual system, better text and density choices, and a more complete conversation interface. Reaction controls still need to reach the published app.

02

Voice across calls and shared spaces

Work in progress connects voice more closely with calls, room conversations and screen sharing. It still needs integration, compatibility review and real-device acceptance.

03

More reliable sharing and recovery

Share lifecycle changes aim to make cancellation, retry and reconnect behavior safer when sessions or routes change. The release candidate must pass focused lifecycle tests before this is described as shipped.

These are release candidates, not promises. Version, package contents and timing will be chosen after integration and stability checks; the current download remains 0.4.0 Preview 1.

Network discovery controls

Receivers can now turn off STUN lookups, router mapping, or both, and a machine-wide override can force that choice for headless use. A smaller-group direction

Longer-term development

Small groups are no longer only a plan. Voice calls, Tables holding several live screens, room discussions and consent-based file transfer all exist in preview, and that page states what each one still cannot do. None of them is presented as a supported workflow, because none has passed physical acceptance across unrelated machines.

The current app still lacks visible reaction controls, message editing and deletion, and unread counts. The coordination service now supports reaction storage and updates, but the published app does not expose that flow yet. Editing, deletion and unread state are not shipped. Recoverable devices and synchronized watching of a local file remain longer-term plans. Packages for macOS, iPhone, iPad and Linux, and screen broadcasting from Android, are absent rather than pending.

Website comparison tables will stay “Not measured” until a public evidence index exists. Help shape the sequence through the issue tracker, without posting invitations or private screen content.
