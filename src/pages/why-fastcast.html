01 / The reason

Control over what
you share

Hosted communication services can retain account, message, and activity records beyond a call. Using a separate screen-sharing tool lets you choose how the screen travels while keeping your existing conversations where they are.

FastCast prefers a direct connection and can fall back to an encrypted media relay. Your devices encode and play the stream, and you choose the capture source and whom to invite. Compare that with what Discord documents.

02

Direct connections and relay fallback

When the devices can reach each other directly, the FastCast relay carries no media. If they need a relay, it forwards encrypted packets while your devices remain the media endpoints. Existing mesh and VPN routes have their own provider terms.

Using FastCast
with your chat app

This preview focuses on sharing a Windows screen with another PC or Android device. It does not replace a hosted community or provide all the messaging and meeting features of Discord or Teams.

You can keep your current chat service and use FastCast for the screen share. The setup guide explains how the viewer sends an invitation to the sharing PC.
