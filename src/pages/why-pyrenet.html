Using {{PRODUCT}}
with your chat app

This preview does carry voice calls, room discussions and file transfer alongside the screen, so a small group of people who already know each other can stay in one place. What it does not do is replace a hosted community: there is no moderation, no searchable history, no invite link anyone can open, and no reactions or message editing. The gaps are listed in full.

You can keep your current chat service and use Cast for the screen share. The setup guide explains how the viewer sends an invitation to the sharing PC.
