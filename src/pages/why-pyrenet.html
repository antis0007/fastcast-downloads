Why Pyrenet

Cast live media

Pyrenet is the shared fabric for private small-group communication and shared computing. Cast is its live-media layer: screen sharing, voice, and remote control that do not require you to move your conversations or community to another service.

01 / The reason

Control over what
you share

Hosted communication services can retain account, message, and activity records beyond a call. Using a separate screen-sharing tool lets you choose how the screen travels while keeping your existing conversations where they are.

Using {{PRODUCT}}
with your chat app

This preview does carry voice calls, room discussions and file transfer alongside the screen, so a small group of people who already know each other can stay in one place. What it does not do is replace a hosted community: there is no moderation, no searchable history, no invite link anyone can open, and no reactions or message editing. The gaps are listed in full.

You can keep your current chat service and use Cast for the screen share. The setup guide explains how the viewer sends an invitation to the sharing PC.
