01 / The reason

Privacy should be
an architecture decision.

Concerned about corporate surveillance? Start by asking what must leave your devices, who can observe it, and how long records can remain. A privacy slogan is not enough.

FastCast starts with one practical piece: screen sharing between machines you control, without a FastCast-hosted workspace or managed media relay. Your hardware encodes and plays back. You choose the source, viewer, and network route. Compare that with what Discord documents.

The current Windows and Android files are free. No paid tier. No billing form. That is today’s offer, not a vow about some future SaaS.

02

No FastCast middlebox.

Media goes over a reachable peer path. If you already run a mesh or a VPN, that is yours. We do not sell traversal.

Claims you can check.

Screenshots are real. Status is written next to each capability. The bandwidth page shows the arithmetic. Discord’s own docs sit next to what this preview actually does.

It is not Discord.

Discord is a hosted social network: serverside chat, communities, Nitro, and an account graph. FastCast is a native screen-sharing utility. No serverside chat. No hosted community. No “it just works on every NAT.”

If that is what you wanted, take the files. If you wanted Discord, you already know where to find it — keep it for chat, use FastCast when you want the stream off their stack.
